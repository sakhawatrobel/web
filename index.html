About

Seeking a U.S. PhD path in advanced simulation and experimental mechanical/materials research.

Robel is a mechanical engineer from Shahjalal University of Science and Technology with hands-on research experience in computer simulation, mechanical design, scientific writing, and data analysis. His work connects atomistic material behavior, thermal systems, and biomedical device mechanics.

He is especially interested in research groups where rigorous modeling meets experimental insight: molecular dynamics of 2D materials and polymer electrolytes, CFD for passive cooling, finite element analysis of biomaterials, and multiphysics simulation for real engineering systems.

Selected Projects

From atomistic simulation to climate-responsive thermal design.

Published CFD Study

Dual-Layer PCM Roofs

Built a 3D transient CFD model using the enthalpy-porosity approach to simulate phase change in a Rubitherm RT-31 roof assembly under Bangladeshi climate conditions.

  • Reduced peak ceiling temperature by up to 6.6 K.
  • Delayed thermal peaks by 2-3 hours.
  • Estimated cooling energy reduction of roughly 30%.
Thesis · Molecular Dynamics

PEO-LiTFSI Polymer Electrolytes

Investigating ionic transport in cross-linked solid polymer electrolytes with LAMMPS and OPLS-AA force-field parameterization.

  • Built linear PEO-LiTFSI systems of about 30,080 atoms.
  • Ran minimization, NPT equilibration, and NVT production.
  • Analyzing RDF, MSD, and ion clustering behavior.
Conference · Nanomaterials

Si-Doped Graphene

Used LAMMPS and Tersoff parameterizations to examine how 1-5% Si doping changes monolayer graphene mechanics and thermal transport.

  • Observed decreasing strength and conductivity with doping.
  • Visualized crack initiation and propagation in OVITO.
  • Validated pristine graphene properties against literature.
Biomedical FEA

Polymeric Heart Valve Leaflets

Contributed CAD modeling and analysis to a reinforced polymeric leaflet study focused on durability and hydrodynamic performance.

  • Supported fiber-reinforcement design evaluation.
  • Improved durability reported up to 73 million AWT cycles.
  • Connected modeling output to device performance goals.
