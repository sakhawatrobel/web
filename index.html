Selected research

Research grounded in simulation depth and engineering outcomes.

Passive cooling · CFD

Dual-Layer PCM Roofs

Developing a 3D CFD model in ANSYS Fluent to evaluate dual-layer PCM roofs in hot-humid climates using climatic data from Dhaka, Cox's Bazar, and Jashore.

Nanomaterials · MD

Doped Graphene

Built LAMMPS models for N, B, B-N, and Si-doped graphene to investigate mechanical strength, stiffness, thermal transport, and fracture mechanisms.

Biomaterials · CAD

Polymeric Heart Valves

Developed SolidWorks CAD models of fiber-reinforced polymeric heart valves and analyzed SEM images of biomaterial particle size using ImageJ.
