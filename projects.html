Published CFD study

Dual-Layer PCM Roofs

Developed a full 3D transient ANSYS Fluent model using the enthalpy-porosity approach for coupled heat conduction, natural convection, and solid-liquid phase change in a Rubitherm RT-31 roof assembly. The model used the hottest recorded conditions in Jashore, Dhaka, and Cox's Bazar and was validated against experimental and EnergyPlus data within 5% deviation.

  • Completed mesh independence across five grid densities.
  • Reduced peak ceiling temperature by 6.6 K, 4.7 K, and 3.25 K across the three cities while delaying peaks by 2-3 hours.
  • Quantified roughly 38% conductive heat-flux reduction and about 30% cooling-energy reduction.
  • Analyzed liquid fraction, temperature contours, and diurnal melting and solidification cycles.

Outcome: Published in Heat Transfer (Wiley), January 2026; related work under review at Building Simulation (Springer).

Molecular dynamics · In progress

PEO-LiTFSI Polymer Electrolytes

Ongoing thesis work on ion transport in cross-linked PEO-LiTFSI solid polymer electrolytes using LAMMPS and the OPLS-AA force field. The study examines Al2O3 nanofillers, Li+-TFSI clustering, and applied initial strain for flexible solid-state lithium-ion batteries.

  • Built linear systems of approximately 30,080 atoms in Python with bonded and non-bonded OPLS-AA parameters.
  • Ran minimization, warm-up, NPT equilibration, and NVT production simulations.
  • Analyzing RDF, MSD, cluster behavior, and ionic conductivity.
  • Resolving Packmol/LAMMPS packing and force-field parameterization ahead of cross-linked and nanofiller models.

Status: In progress with Kamrul Hasan.

Conference · Nanomaterials

Si-Doped Graphene

Conducted classical molecular dynamics simulations in LAMMPS to examine how 1-5% silicon doping changes ultimate tensile strength, fracture strain, Young's modulus, and thermal conductivity using 1989 and 1994 Tersoff parameterizations.

  • Found systematic decreases in mechanical strength and thermal conductivity with increasing Si concentration.
  • Used OVITO to show fractures nucleating at Si-C bonds.
  • Validated pristine graphene properties against published literature.

Outcome: Presented at ICME 2025 at BUET, Dhaka; available on SSRN.

Biomedical FEA

Polymeric Heart Valve Leaflets

Contributed to a Colorado State University and Georgia Institute of Technology study using ABAQUS finite element analysis to optimize fiber reinforcement in polymeric transcatheter aortic valve leaflets.

  • Evaluated strong, weld-like fibers at the commissure post.
  • Improved durability from a few million to 73 million accelerated wear-test cycles.
  • Maintained minimal change to hydrodynamic performance.

Outcome: Published in the Journal of the Mechanical Behavior of Biomedical Materials, January 2025.
