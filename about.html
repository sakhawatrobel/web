Profile

Curiosity grounded in rigorous modeling.

I am a mechanical engineer with a strong foundation in computational simulation, specializing in mechanical design and modeling, computational fluid dynamics, molecular dynamics, and finite element analysis. My research experience spans nanomaterials, polymer electrolytes, electrochemistry, biomaterials, fluid-structure interaction, phase change materials, and passive cooling technologies.

Across multiple undergraduate research roles, I developed research methodology, literature review, scientific writing, technical presentation, and problem-solving skills. I am seeking an experimental and advanced simulation-driven mechanical or materials PhD opportunity to grow as an independent researcher and contribute meaningfully to science.
