Profile

Connecting rigorous modeling with engineering insight.

Robel is a mechanical engineer from Shahjalal University of Science and Technology with hands-on research experience in computer simulation, mechanical design, scientific writing, and data analysis.

He is especially interested in molecular dynamics of 2D materials and polymer electrolytes, CFD for passive cooling, finite element analysis of biomaterials, and multiphysics simulation for real engineering systems.
